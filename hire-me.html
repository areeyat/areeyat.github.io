data cleaning & automation

Got a messy spreadsheet with typos, duplicates, and messed-up formatting? I'll clean it up and give it back in a format you'll actually use. Then, if you're spending hours every week copying, pasting, and reformatting the same data, I'll set it up so the spreadsheet does that work for you.

  • typos, duplicates, and inconsistent entries
  • mixed-up dates, names, phone numbers, and addresses
  • data split across multiple files or tabs
  • broken formulas and messy formatting
  • spreadsheets that grew over years without a plan

you'll get

  • your cleaned, organized data
  • a summary of what I changed and why
  • recommendations for data entry so it stays clean
  • a tidy template to use going forward

automate the repetitive stuff

  • combine weekly exports into one sheet automatically
  • auto-generated totals, summaries, and reports
  • dropdowns and checks that stop bad data at entry
  • alerts when stock is low or a number looks off
  • forms that feed straight into your spreadsheet
  • a walkthrough so you know how it all works
